Neighborhood errands study

A short research survey about small, local, physical tasks—pickups, drops, queues, documents, pharmacy items, and similar errands near where you live or work.

Why we are asking

We are running a local research exercise to learn how often people run into these situations, what they do today, and what would feel fair and safe if paid help were available. We are not selling a product on this page, and no app install or payment is required to answer.

Which survey should I take?

I sometimes need help

Choose this if you occasionally need someone nearby for a small errand (for example market or medicine pickup, document drop, forgotten item, queue or token help, or a short local run).

Start: need help

I might help others

Choose this if you might be open to occasionally helping others nearby for a fee, when the task, distance, time, and safety feel right. Any future research pilot would let you choose tasks; nothing here is a job offer.

Start: might help

Privacy

Responses are used for research and planning only. Each Google Form describes what is collected when you start the survey.
